# A Love Token for Future DMs

Before we continue…

This is a small love token to future DMs who, like me when I started, do not come from software engineering.

I built this because I wanted to spend less time worrying about repositories, databases, broken saves, and whether an AI remembered everything correctly—and more time enjoying the game with my friends.

You do not need to understand every mechanism behind it.

You only need to tell the AI **what** you want to build.  
The AI will propose the plan.  
This safety layer will help protect **how** it is built.

If something goes wrong, the purpose of this system is not to punish experimentation. It is to give you a safe place to try, inspect, learn, and return without sacrificing the campaign you have already played.

I hope it makes your life a little easier—and leaves you with more time for the part that actually matters:

**playing the world you came here to create.**

Your installation also includes a reusable safety prompt for future changes:

**[Open the LotS Safe Build Prompt](LOTS_SAFE_BUILD_PROMPT.md)**

Keep it with your campaign and use it whenever you want to build, change, or safely remove a system. You do not need to understand the engineering underneath it—the prompt will help the AI translate your idea into a protected plan.

Take care of your campaign. ❤️  
— Nollie
